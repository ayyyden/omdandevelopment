Common questions

How much will my project cost?

It depends on the size of the area, the base preparation it needs and the materials you choose. That's why we visit first: you get personalized pricing in a clear written quote after a free on-site estimate.

Does artificial turf hold up in desert heat?

Quality turf is made for strong sun, and a good install with proper base prep and drainage is what makes it last. We'll go over turf options suited to your yard, pets and how you use the space.

My HOA has rules. Can you still do turf?

Many Coachella Valley HOAs allow artificial turf with guidelines on look and placement. Bring your HOA's rules to the estimate and we'll plan the project around them.

Pavers or concrete: which should I choose?

Pavers give you more looks and are easy to lift and reset in sections. Concrete, including stamped concrete, gives a seamless surface. We'll show you both at the estimate and help you pick what fits your home.

Do you do repairs?

For outdoor work we focus on new installations and full front or back yard remodels, not repairs.

What areas do you serve?

Palm Springs, Cathedral City, Rancho Mirage, Thousand Palms, Palm Desert, Indian Wells, La Quinta, Indio, Coachella, Desert Hot Springs, Morongo Valley, Yucca Valley, Joshua Tree, Twentynine Palms, Cabazon, Banning, Cherry Valley and Yucaipa.
