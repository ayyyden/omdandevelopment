Handyman services in Palm Springs

Common repairs and installs we handle throughout Palm Springs:

Drywall Repair

Patch holes, fix damage from water or hardware, match texture, and finish to paint-ready condition. Common between rentals and after any wall work.

Door & Hardware Repairs

Door adjustments, latch and lock repairs, deadbolt replacement, slider lock fixes, and cabinet hardware swaps. Common items on rental punch lists.

Fixture Replacement

Faucet swaps, showerhead replacement, toilet hardware, light fixture installs, and ceiling fan replacement. Clean installs with no visible damage to surrounding surfaces.

Tile & Floor Touch-Ups

Replace cracked or loose tiles, regrout worn grout joints, repair flooring transitions, and fix trip hazards on patios, walkways, and pool areas.

Exterior Repairs

Stucco crack repair, fascia work, breezeblock repair and repaint, gate and fence hardware, and caulking around windows and doors — all common from desert heat and UV exposure.

Small Carpentry

Shelf installation, cabinet door realignment, trim repair, baseboards, and small woodworking repairs that make a property feel well-maintained.

Rental Punch Lists

Multiple repair items handled in one efficient visit. We work between guest stays to keep your rental in top condition without disrupting your booking calendar.

Pre-Listing Repairs

Address the repair list before you list your property — drywall, doors, fixtures, caulking, and cosmetic fixes that help your home show well and avoid buyer negotiations.

Common repairs in Palm Springs properties

Desert conditions create specific, recurring repair needs:

  • Sun-faded fascia, stucco cracks, and caulking failures from extreme UV and temperature cycles
  • Pool deck trip hazards, loose coping stones, and cracked deck tile
  • Sliding door and slider lock repairs — a top rental complaint item
  • Breezeblock wall repair and repaint in mid-century properties
  • Drywall holes and texture damage between tenants or after contractor work
  • Faucet and fixture failures from hard mineral-rich Coachella Valley water
  • A/C condensate drainage issues common with flat mid-century roofs
  • Gate and fence hardware replacement from rust and UV exposure

Palm Springs handyman FAQ

What handyman services do you provide in Palm Springs?

We handle a wide range of home repairs and installs including drywall repair, door and hardware repairs, fixture replacement, tile and floor touch-ups, small carpentry, exterior repairs, fence and gate work, and general property maintenance. We also handle rental punch lists and pre-listing repairs.

Do you help with rental property punch lists?

Yes. Rental punch lists are a significant part of our Palm Springs work. We coordinate scheduling around guest check-in and check-out windows, work efficiently to minimize vacancy time, and handle multiple repair items in a single visit rather than requiring separate trips for each task.

Can you help with drywall, doors, fixtures, and small repairs?

Yes. Common repairs include drywall patching and texture matching, door adjustments and lock repairs, faucet and fixture replacement, cabinet hardware swaps, caulking and sealing, and small carpentry repairs. Send photos and we'll confirm scope.

Do you work with condos, HOAs, and vacation homes?

Yes. We regularly work in condos, HOA communities, and vacation homes throughout Palm Springs. We supply scope documentation and photos for HOA submissions, work within building access hours, and coordinate around rental bookings.

Can you work between guest stays?

Yes — send the booking window and we'll stage materials so the punch list wraps before the next check-in. We understand the Palm Springs vacation rental market and work efficiently to minimize vacancy.

Do you handle vintage fixtures?
