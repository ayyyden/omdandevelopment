Handyman services in Cathedral City

Common repairs and installs we handle throughout Cathedral City:

Drywall Repair

Patch and texture-match damage in aging condo walls, repair holes from relocated fixtures or hardware, and finish to paint-ready condition. Older drywall in 1970s buildings often needs texture matching that requires experience to get right.

Door & Hardware Repairs

Door adjustments, latch and lock repairs, deadbolt replacement, and cabinet hardware swaps. Decades of use in 1970s–80s condo units means door frames have shifted and hardware is frequently worn or failing.

Fixture Replacement

Faucet swaps, showerhead replacement, toilet hardware, light fixture installs, and ceiling fan replacement. Original 1970s–80s fixtures in Cathedral City units are frequently at end of life and replacement is straightforward.

Tile & Grout Repairs

Replace cracked tiles, regrout aged grout joints, and fix caulking around tubs and showers. Common in older Cathedral City condos where original tile has been in place since the 1970s and grout has deteriorated.

Small Carpentry

Shelf installation, cabinet door realignment, trim repair, baseboards, and small woodworking repairs. In MCM homes in Cathedral Canyon, we source materials and hardware appropriate to the home's period.

Exterior Repairs

Stucco crack repair, caulking around windows and doors, screen repairs, and gate hardware. Cathedral City's desert climate accelerates surface wear — especially on west- and south-facing exposures.

Rental Punch Lists

Cathedral City has a growing rental market. We handle multi-item punch lists for condo owners preparing units for new tenants, and for STR hosts scheduling repairs between bookings.

Pre-Listing Repairs

Address the repair list before listing your Cathedral City property — drywall, doors, fixtures, caulking, and cosmetic fixes that help a unit show well and avoid buyer negotiations at close.

Common repairs in Cathedral City properties

Age, climate, and building type create specific recurring repair patterns in Cathedral City:

  • Drywall damage from decades of aging in 1970s–80s condo units — settling cracks, nail pops, and hole repairs
  • Door frame shifting in older condo buildings causing latch misalignment and sticking
  • Original bathroom and kitchen fixtures that have reached end of life after 40+ years of use
  • Dried and cracked caulking around tubs, showers, and windows in older units
  • Cabinet hardware worn, broken, or missing in kitchens and bathrooms throughout the building
  • Grout deterioration in original 1970s tile bathrooms — staining, cracking, and separation
  • Breezeblock detail repair and repaint on MCM single-family homes in Cathedral Canyon
  • Stucco cracks and exterior caulking failure from pass-wind exposure and temperature cycles

Cathedral City handyman FAQ
