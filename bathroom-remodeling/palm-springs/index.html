Bathroom remodeling services in Palm Springs

We handle the full range of bathroom remodel work for residential properties in Palm Springs — from targeted upgrades to complete teardowns and rebuilds.

Shower Remodels

Shower remodels are the most common bathroom project we do in Palm Springs. We remove old tile, waterproof the substrate correctly, and install new tile in the pattern and finish that fits the design. Common upgrades include niche shelves, linear drains, frameless glass, and larger-format tile that reduces grout lines and looks cleaner. For older Palm Springs homes with original shower surrounds, we work carefully around adjacent walls and plumbing.

Tub-to-Shower Conversions

Many Palm Springs homeowners — especially vacation rental owners — want to remove an old tub and convert the space into a walk-in shower. We handle the demo, drain relocation if needed, waterproofing, and full tile install. A curbless shower or low-threshold entry adds a modern feel and works well for guests of all ages.

Tile Work

Floor tile, wall tile, shower surrounds, backsplashes, and decorative accents. We work with a range of tile formats — from small mosaic to large-format porcelain and natural stone. For mid-century homes, we can match or complement existing terrazzo and decorative tile. For contemporary upgrades, large-format tiles with minimal grout lines give a clean, modern look that photographs well for rental listings. See our tile work service page for more detail.

Vanity Replacement

Replacing a dated vanity cabinet, countertop, sink, and faucet is one of the faster wins in a bathroom remodel. We handle demo of the old unit, any drywall repair behind it, and installation of the new vanity with proper plumbing connections. Floating vanities, double-sink configurations, and updated mirrors and lighting can all be included. For vacation rentals, we choose finishes that hold up to repeated guest use.

Bathroom Flooring

We remove old flooring, prep the substrate, and install new tile or LVP flooring in the bathroom. For Palm Springs homes, porcelain tile is the most practical choice — it handles moisture, desert temperature swings, and heavy use. We can match the floor to the shower and wall tile for a cohesive look, or go with a contrasting floor for a more deliberate design. See our floor services page for related work.

Drywall, Waterproofing & Finish Work

Behind every good tile job is properly waterproofed substrate. We use cement board and appropriate waterproofing membranes in wet areas so there are no moisture issues behind the finish. Any drywall patching and painting needed as part of the remodel is included. We also add proper ventilation when it's lacking — desert humidity in shower rooms is real even in a dry climate. See our drywall repair service for standalone patching work.

Plumbing Coordination

When a bathroom remodel requires moving supply lines, changing drain locations, or upgrading the shower valve and trim, we coordinate licensed plumbing support as part of the project. We work with plumbing crews to make sure the rough-in is done before tile starts, so the finished project is built right.

Remodeling bathrooms in Palm Springs homes, rentals & condos

Palm Springs has a concentration of older homes — mid-century modern, Spanish-style, and desert ranch builds from the 1950s through 1970s — where bathrooms often have original tile, original plumbing trim, and tight layouts that reflect the architecture of that era. These homes need a contractor who understands what to keep and what to improve without breaking the character of the space.

Vacation rentals and investment properties in neighborhoods like Vista Las Palmas, Deepwell, and South Palm Springs have a different priority: durable, attractive, easy to clean, and able to handle repeated guest use. Grout lines that stain easily, fixtures that break down, and flooring that shows wear quickly are problems we plan around. We choose materials and finishes that hold up while still looking good in listing photos.

For condos and HOA properties, we follow building access requirements, protect common areas, and coordinate work to avoid impacting neighboring units. Dust containment and organized staging are part of how we operate on any job site.

Materials that work well in Palm Springs bathrooms

  • Terrazzo-look and large-format porcelain that pairs naturally with mid-century breezeblock details and lines
  • Brushed brass and matte black fixtures that update the look without clashing with mid-century palettes
  • Low-iron frameless glass panels to keep sightlines open in smaller bathrooms
  • Porcelain floor tile that handles moisture, heat, and repeated cleaning without showing wear quickly
  • Solid-surface or quartz vanity tops that resist staining and are easy to wipe clean between guests

Signs it may be time to remodel your Palm Springs bathroom

  • Tile grout is stained, cracked, or separating from the wall — a sign of moisture intrusion that worsens over time
  • The vanity cabinet is swollen, stained, or the countertop is chipped and shows its age in photos
  • The shower pan or tub is cracked, stained, or shows discoloration that cleaning no longer removes
  • Fixtures are corroded, outdated, or difficult to source replacement parts for
  • Guest or renter feedback consistently mentions the bathroom as a weak point in an otherwise well-maintained property

Bathroom remodel timeline in Palm Springs

  1. 1. Design & selections (days 1–4): Confirm tile, glass, vanity, and fixture packages around your rental schedule or availability. We walk through options and make sure everything is ordered before demo starts.
  2. 2. Demo & rough-in (days 5–8): Remove existing tile, fixtures, and flooring. Inspect substrate. Handle any plumbing or electrical rough-in work. Waterproof wet areas and prep for tile.
  3. 3. Tile, fixtures & finishes (days 9–14): Set tile, install glass, vanity, mirrors, lighting, and accessories. Final walkthrough with you, site cleaned, and bathroom ready to use.

Timeline varies by scope. Larger remodels or those involving layout changes take longer. We give you a clear schedule upfront.

Common bathroom fixes in Palm Springs homes

  • Smoothing hairline cracks in original plaster walls before new tile or paint
  • Re-sealing terrazzo or concrete floors after shower demo without damaging the surrounding material
  • Updating vintage plumbing trim while keeping finishes that match the home's original character
  • Adding quiet exhaust ventilation — important in desert homes where shower steam lingers and causes moisture issues over time
  • Replacing stained or cracked floor tile in pool house and casita bathrooms that see heavy outdoor-to-indoor traffic

Bathroom remodel FAQ — Palm Springs

Do you remodel small bathrooms in Palm Springs?

Yes. Small bathrooms — including half baths, hall baths, and the compact bathrooms common in older Palm Springs homes and casitas — are a regular part of our work. We focus on efficient layouts, appropriate tile scale, and finishes that make a small space feel clean and functional.

Can you help with shower remodels and tile work?

Yes. We remove existing tile, waterproof the substrate properly, and install new tile in the pattern you choose. We also do tub-to-shower conversions. Tile selection, layout, and grout color are all part of the planning conversation.

Do you replace vanities, flooring, and fixtures?

Yes. Vanity replacement, bathroom flooring, mirrors, lighting, and fixture upgrades are all within our scope. These can be done as standalone updates or as part of a full remodel. Targeted upgrades like a new vanity and flooring can meaningfully improve the look of a rental bathroom without a full demo.

Can you remodel bathrooms in vacation rentals or condos?

Yes. We work with vacation rental owners and property managers throughout Palm Springs regularly. We schedule around occupancy windows and work efficiently. For condos and HOA properties, we follow building access protocols and keep the job site organized.

Do you help coordinate plumbing-related bathroom work?

Yes. When a remodel requires moving lines, changing drain locations, or upgrading shower valves, we coordinate licensed plumbing support as part of the project. Plumbing rough-in happens before tile starts so the finished project is built right.

Can you remodel between guest stays?

Yes. Share your block-out window and we'll stage materials and plan the sequence so the bathroom is rebuilt, cleaned, and ready before the next check-in.

How do I request a bathroom remodeling estimate?

Call us at (951) 292-0703 or submit a request online. Photos and a description of what you want to change help us respond faster with relevant questions and a free estimate.
