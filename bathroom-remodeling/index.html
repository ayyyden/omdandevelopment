Before you start

Planning a Bathroom Remodel in a Desert Home

Waterproofing first

Behind every new shower is a waterproof membrane and a properly sloped pan. It is the part you never see and the part that decides whether the tile lasts.

Hard water

Mineral-heavy water leaves scale on glass, fixtures, and grout. Fixture finishes, glass coatings, and an optional softener are worth deciding early.

Ventilation

Even in a dry climate, a bathroom needs a correctly sized, ducted exhaust fan. See why bathroom ventilation matters more in the desert.

Accessibility and aging in place

Curbless showers, grab-bar blocking, comfort-height toilets, and wider doorways are easiest to add during a remodel.

FAQ

Bathroom remodeling questions we hear weekly

How soon can a cosmetic refresh start?

Most cosmetic work (vanities, fixtures, tile accents) starts within 3–5 business days once selections are confirmed. Larger remodels are scheduled as soon as materials land.

Do you cover Menifee, Murrieta, Temecula, and San Bernardino?

Yes. Dedicated crews cover the Inland Empire and Coachella Valley—share the zip code and we’ll confirm availability before booking.

Can you bundle bath remodels with other scopes?

We often pair bath remodels with nearby scopes (paint, flooring, turf/paver upgrades) so you only coordinate one contractor and schedule.

What affects the cost of a bathroom remodel?

The cost depends on your home: the size of the bathroom, tile and fixture selections, moving plumbing or walls, a custom shower or tub, and permits. Because no two bathrooms are the same, we don't publish one-size-fits-all prices. We'll visit your home, measure, and give you personalized pricing with a free on-site estimate. Call or text (951) 292-0703.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

A vanity or fixture update is much shorter than a full remodel with a new shower. We give you a timeline with your free on-site estimate, and the schedule is confirmed once materials are ordered.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. When the scope requires a permit, such as moving plumbing, adding circuits, or changing walls, we pull it and coordinate the inspections.
