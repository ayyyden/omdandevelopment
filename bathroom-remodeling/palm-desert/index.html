Signs it's time to remodel your Palm Desert bathroom

  • Grout lines that trap mineral deposits and won't come clean
  • Shower glass that's sun-faded or etched from hard water
  • Fixtures that no longer match an updated kitchen or flooring
  • An outdated tub that guests don't use — convert it to a walk-in shower
  • Tile cracks or lippage from slab shifts common in desert soil
  • Planning to sell or increase the rental rate for your vacation property

Typical home fixes in Palm Desert

  • Replacing sun-faded shower glass that overlooks courtyards
  • Leveling slab dips before laying large-format tile
  • Quiet exhaust upgrades that meet HOA sound limits
  • Coordinating soft-water hookups for new fixtures

Palm Desert bathroom remodel FAQ

Can you work while we're out of town?

Do you handle HOA submissions?

Will carts or golf paths stay clean?

What neighborhoods do you serve in Palm Desert?
