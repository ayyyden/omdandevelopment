Kitchen upgrades for Palm Springs homes, condos, and rentals

Palm Springs has a mix of property types that each come with specific considerations for kitchen remodels:

  • Mid-century single-family homes — Many kitchens in neighborhoods like Movie Colony and Old Las Palmas have original 1950s–70s layouts with galley or U-shaped kitchens, older plumbing, and non-standard cabinet sizes. We work with what's there rather than creating unnecessary scope.
  • Condos and HOA communities — Condo kitchen work often requires HOA approval, work-hour restrictions, and elevator/building access coordination. We provide scope documentation and photos for HOA submissions and schedule accordingly.
  • Vacation rentals and Airbnbs — Palm Springs has one of the largest short-term rental markets in California. We schedule around booking calendars, prioritize durable materials that hold up to high turnover, and work efficiently to minimize vacancy time.
  • Desert climate considerations — Extreme heat cycles affect cabinet materials, adhesives, and caulking. We use materials appropriate for the desert environment and ensure joints and transitions are finished to handle temperature swings.

Signs it may be time to remodel your Palm Springs kitchen

Cabinets are warped, worn, or outdated with no hardware updates
Countertops are chipped, cracked, or stained beyond cleaning
Tile grout is deteriorating or backsplash looks dated
Flooring is lifting, cracked, or mismatched from renovations over the years
Rental guests or buyers comment on the kitchen as a negative
Plumbing fixtures are outdated, corroded, or leaking at seals
