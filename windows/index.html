Window Services We Offer

From single-pane swaps to full-home upgrades.

Single-Pane Replacement

Old single-pane windows let desert heat straight in. We swap them for dual-pane low-E glass to cut cooling costs.

Slider & Casement Windows

Smooth-operating frames with tight seals that hold up to wind and dust common in the Coachella Valley.

Bay & Picture Windows

Large-view windows installed plumb, level, and properly sealed for a clean interior and exterior finish.

Custom Sizes

Non-standard openings cut and fitted with the correct frame — no gaps, no slop, no visible filler.

Security Upgrades

Impact-resistant glass and reinforced frames for improved home security and added peace of mind.

Screen & Hardware Replacement

Worn screens, broken operators, and old locks swapped in the same visit as the window install.
