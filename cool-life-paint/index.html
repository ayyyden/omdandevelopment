Keep the desert heat off your roof — and out of your home

Cool Life elastomeric coating reflects solar radiation instead of absorbing it. In Palm Springs, where summer surface temps can exceed 160°F, a reflective coating is one of the most cost-effective energy improvements you can make.

  • • Lowers roof surface temps significantly in desert heat
  • • Reduces cooling load and AC runtime
  • • Extends roof membrane life by reducing thermal cycling
  • • White, light gray, and tan color options

Combining with roofing or insulation?

Cool Life coating works best when paired with proper attic insulation and a solid roof membrane. We handle all three in one project.

Cool Life Coating Applications

Every surface type has a right approach — we assess before we apply.

Flat Roof Coating

Seamless elastomeric coating applied to flat TPO, foam, or built-up roofs. Full surface coverage with no seams for water to penetrate.

Low-Slope Application

Coverage on pitches under 3:12 where standard shingles aren't appropriate. Proper primer and multiple-coat application for full adhesion.

Metal Roof Coating

Reflective paint applied over metal panels to cut radiant heat transfer into the structure below — especially effective on garages and carports.

Concrete Surface Sealing

Exterior concrete flat work sealed with a cool-color coating to reduce ground-level heat gain around the building perimeter.

Recoat & Refresh

Inspection and recoating of existing applications past their warranty period. We clean, repair cracks, and apply a fresh reflective layer.

Color Selection

White, light gray, and tan cool-roof colors to match your home exterior. We bring samples so you can see the finish before committing.
