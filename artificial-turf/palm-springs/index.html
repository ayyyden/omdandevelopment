Turf that fits mid-century and modern homes

In Palm Springs, the best turf projects often use less turf, not more. A wall-to-wall lawn can look out of place next to a flat-roofed modern home. These layouts tend to work better:

  • Turf joints: strips of turf between large-format pavers or concrete slabs, laid out on a grid
  • A single crisp rectangle of turf framed by decomposed granite or gravel
  • Turf for the play or pet area only, with desert plants, boulders, and rock for the rest of the yard
  • Straight, clean edges. Steel or concrete borders keep the lines sharp.

We can handle the turf, pavers, concrete, and gravel as one project, so the pieces line up.

Turf for Palm Springs vacation rentals

A rental yard can get more wear in a month than a family yard gets in a year, and it still has to look good in the listing. A few choices make a big difference:

  • A durable turf rated for heavy foot traffic in the areas guests use most
  • Permeable pet turf if the property allows dogs
  • A clean border between turf and the pool deck so debris doesn't track into the water
  • A simple routine for your property manager: blow off debris, rinse, and brush high-traffic areas
  • Scheduling between bookings. Tell us your blocked dates at the estimate and we'll plan around them.

Wind in north Palm Springs

Neighborhoods on the north side of Palm Springs, closer to the San Gorgonio Pass, get some of the strongest wind in the valley. Wind doesn't damage well-installed turf, but it finds weak spots:

  • Seams need to be glued and secured, not just tacked
  • Perimeter edges should be fastened closely so gusts can't get under them
  • Sand and dust blow into the fibers. A stiff brush and a rinse after a windstorm restores the look.
  • Turf next to open desert lots collects more debris, and a wall, hedge, or gravel buffer helps

Turf around pools

Turf beside the pool is one of the most-requested looks in Palm Springs. Done well, it's soft and clean. Done badly, it sends sand and debris into the water. What we plan for:

  • Grading the base so rinse water and rain drain away from the pool, not into it
  • A clean, secured edge where the turf meets the deck
  • Infill that stays put when it gets wet
  • Clear access to pool equipment

Desert Water Agency and rebates

Most Palm Springs homes get their water from Desert Water Agency (DWA). DWA has offered conservation and turf-replacement programs, but the terms change, and some don't count artificial turf. Most also require approval before grass is removed. If a rebate matters to you, check DWA's current program at dwa.org before you start.

Frequently asked questions: Palm Springs turf

Does artificial turf work with mid-century landscaping?

Yes, especially in small, geometric doses: turf between pavers, a framed rectangle of green, or a defined pet or play area surrounded by gravel and desert plants. Clean, straight edges matter more than square footage.

Is artificial turf a good choice for a Palm Springs vacation rental?

Usually. It stays green without irrigation, handles guest traffic, and looks good in listing photos. Choose a durable turf for high-traffic areas, use pet turf if the rental allows dogs, and give your property manager a simple cleaning routine.

Will wind in north Palm Springs damage artificial turf?

Not if it's installed well. Strong wind finds weak seams and loose edges, so both need to be secured closely. Expect sand and dust in the fibers after a windstorm. Brushing and rinsing restores the look.

Can you install turf around my pool?

Yes. We grade the base so water drains away from the pool, secure the edge where turf meets the deck, use infill that stays put when wet, and keep pool equipment accessible.

Does DWA offer rebates for artificial turf?

Desert Water Agency's programs change over time, and some don't count artificial turf. Most require approval before grass is removed. Check DWA's current program before you start.

Does artificial turf get hot in Palm Springs?

Yes. In full summer afternoon sun, it gets too hot for bare feet. Shade from the house, patio covers, or trees, cooling infill options, and a quick rinse all help. We'll show you which parts of your yard suit turf.

Can you install pet turf?

Yes. Pet areas use fully permeable turf over a free-draining compacted base, with odor-control infill and edges secured so dogs can't pull them up. Rinse the potty area a few times a week.

What affects the cost of artificial turf in Palm Springs?

The cost depends on your yard: the size of the area, removal of existing grass or rock, base preparation and drainage, the turf product (lawn, pet, playground, or putting green), and edging.
