Frequently Asked Questions

Does artificial turf get too hot in Moreno Valley summers?

Turf does heat up in direct sun — this is common in any hot climate including Moreno Valley. We can discuss infill options and placement strategies to reduce surface temperature. A quick rinse with the hose cools it down fast.

How does drainage work with artificial turf?

We prepare the base with proper grading and a decomposed granite or aggregate layer so water drains through quickly. Standing water should not be an issue with a properly installed system.
