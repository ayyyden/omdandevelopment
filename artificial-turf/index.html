Pet Turf, Playground Turf & Putting Greens

Three specialty installs we do for homeowners. Each one needs a different turf product and a different base.

Pet Turf for Dogs

Dog areas fail when urine sits in the base. Pet turf uses a fully permeable backing over a compacted base that drains, so the surface can be rinsed clean instead of holding odor.

  • Drainage-focused base under dog runs and potty areas
  • Odor-control infill options instead of plain sand
  • Secured edges along fences so dogs can't dig under
  • Shade planning, because any turf heats up in desert sun

Local notes: dog turf in Palm Desert and dog turf in Desert Hot Springs.

Playground Turf

Backyard play areas need a soft, non-abrasive turf and a level, well-drained base. Under swing sets and climbers, turf is usually installed over a cushioning pad.

  • Soft-blade turf suited to kids' play
  • Padding options under play equipment, reviewed at your estimate
  • No mud, no sprinklers, no bare patches under swings
  • Heat tips for summer: shade, afternoon rinse, cooling infill

Read more: Is artificial turf safe for kids and pets in hot climates?

Backyard Putting Greens

A putting green uses a short, dense putting turf over a base that's shaped for gentle breaks, then trimmed into a fringe of landscape turf so it blends with the yard.

  • Shaped and compacted base for a true roll
  • Cup placement planned around your layout
  • Fringe and edging that transition into lawn turf
  • Can be combined with a pet or lawn area in one project

How We Install Artificial Turf

Most turf problems start under the turf. Here is what happens on a typical residential install.

  1. 1. Free on-site estimate

    We measure the area, check slope and drainage, locate sprinkler lines, and talk through how you'll use the yard: dogs, kids, putting, or a clean low-water lawn.

  2. 2. Removal and grading

    Existing grass, rock, or dirt is removed, irrigation in the turf area is capped or converted, and the ground is graded so water runs away from the house.

  3. 3. Compacted base

    A crushed-rock base is spread and compacted in layers. This is what keeps the surface flat and draining for years, especially under pet areas.

  4. 4. Turf, seams, and edges

    Turf is rolled out with the grain running the same direction, seams are joined so they don't show, and edges are secured against borders, pavers, and walls.

  5. 5. Infill, brushing, and cleanup

    The right infill for your use goes in, the blades are brushed upright, and the site is cleaned before we walk the finished yard with you.

Artificial Turf FAQ

What affects the cost of artificial turf installation?

The cost depends on your yard: the size of the area, removal of existing grass or rock and grading, base depth and drainage, the turf product (lawn, pet, playground, or putting green), and edging or borders. Because no two yards are the same, we don't publish one-size-fits-all prices. We'll visit your home, measure, and give you personalized pricing with a free on-site estimate. Call or text (951) 292-0703.

What types of artificial turf do you install?

We install residential lawn turf, pet turf for dogs, playground turf for kids' play areas, and backyard putting greens, plus turf around pools and patios. Each one uses a turf product and base suited to how the area is used.

Is artificial turf good for dogs?

Yes, when it's built for pets. We install pet turf with a fully permeable backing over a compacted base that drains, and offer odor-control infill options. Rinsing the dog area regularly keeps it clean, and shade helps in summer because any turf heats up in direct desert sun.

Do you install playground turf?

Yes. We install soft-blade playground turf for backyard play areas. Under swing sets and climbers, turf is usually installed over a cushioning pad; we'll go over padding options for your play equipment during the on-site estimate.

Do you build backyard putting greens?

Yes. Putting greens use a short, dense putting turf over a shaped and compacted base, with cups placed around your layout and a fringe of landscape turf so the green blends into the yard.

How hot does artificial turf get in the desert?

Artificial turf gets hotter than natural grass in direct summer sun in the Coachella Valley. Shade, cooling infill, lighter turf colors, and a quick rinse on hot afternoons all help. We'll talk through heat for your yard's sun exposure during the estimate.

How long does artificial turf last?

Quality artificial turf typically lasts 15–20 years with minimal maintenance. We use durable, UV-resistant products suited to the Southern California climate.

Which cities do you serve for artificial turf?
