Roof Reset

Roof Reset Project in Agoura Hills, CA

Agoura Hills, CA April 17, 2026 17 photos

About This Project

In April 2026, a homeowner in Agoura Hills reached out to Omdan Development for help with a long-overdue exterior project. The property had sustained minor storm-related displacement at the roofline — not a structural failure, but the kind of surface-level issue that worsens over time when left unaddressed. The homeowner needed a reliable team to handle the reset work cleanly, document it thoroughly, and restore the exterior's integrity before the problem spread.

Omdan Development handled this project as part of our broader exterior improvement and repair services. We are not a roofing contractor — our scope here was exterior prep, surface-level correction, and site documentation: the kind of hands-on support work that a general contractor performs when a home needs its exterior brought back to a clean, solid baseline.

What the Work Involved

Before any work began, the crew performed a full walkthrough of the affected areas and photographed existing conditions for the homeowner's records. This documentation step is standard practice on every Omdan Development project. It protects both the homeowner and our team, and it gives the property owner a clear before-and-after record that can support insurance correspondence or future reference.

The work itself focused on resetting displaced material, cleaning and clearing the surrounding areas, and ensuring the roofline presented a clean, uniform appearance from every angle. All debris was removed from the work zones before the crew left the property. The homeowner's yard, driveway, and surrounding landscaping were returned to the same condition as when the project began.

Total time on site: one day. The 17 photos in this project gallery document the full scope of the work — from initial site conditions through the completed reset. Nothing was staged after the fact. This is a straight record of how the property looked and how the project progressed from start to finish.
