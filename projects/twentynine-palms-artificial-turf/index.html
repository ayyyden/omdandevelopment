About This Project

Desert Green Builders completed this artificial turf installation in Twentynine Palms, California. The project transformed the outdoor area into a clean, durable, and low-maintenance space with professionally installed artificial grass.

Twentynine Palms is one of the driest and hottest communities in the high desert — a landscape where natural grass requires significant irrigation and struggles to stay healthy year-round. Artificial turf is a practical, long-term solution that stays green and clean through the heat without water, fertilizer, or mowing.

What the Work Involved

  • Site Preparation — Existing surface cleared and graded to ensure proper drainage and a stable, level base before turf installation.
  • Base Installation — Compacted aggregate base laid to support the turf and prevent shifting or uneven settling over time.
  • Artificial Turf Installation — High-quality artificial grass cut, fitted, and secured with clean, finished edges throughout the project area.
