Tract homes, water heaters and slow leaks in Moreno Valley

Many Moreno Valley homes were built in the 1980s, 90s and 2000s. That makes them old enough for the original water heaters, washer hoses, toilet supply lines and window seals to start failing. Most of the mold we find here comes from one of those slow leaks, usually in a garage, a closet or under a sink.

Stucco cracks and sprinklers aimed at the walls add another source from the outside. We find the water, repair it, remove the mold and damaged materials, and rebuild so you're left with a finished room, not open walls.

Where we find mold in Moreno Valley homes

  • Garage and closet water heaters: A leak at the base of an older water heater soaks the drywall and the bottom plate of the wall.
  • Washer and toilet supply lines: Hoses and lines that are 15 or 20 years old can leak or burst without warning.
  • Stucco cracks and sprinklers: Water from sprinklers and rain can get in through cracks and reach the framing.
  • Upstairs bathrooms: A leaking shower pan or toilet seal upstairs shows up as a stain on the ceiling below.

How we handle mold in Moreno Valley

1. Inspect and test

We look at the affected area, take moisture readings to see how far the water went, and test when it helps answer a question. If you already have a report from an independent inspector, we work from it.

2. Find and fix the water

Mold comes back if the moisture does. We find the leak, condensation or drainage problem and fix it as part of the job.

3. Contain and remove

We seal off the work area, remove moldy drywall, insulation and other porous materials, and clean the remaining surfaces with HEPA vacuums and an antimicrobial treatment.

4. Dry, check and rebuild

We confirm the area is dry before closing it up, then rebuild: drywall, insulation, paint, baseboards, cabinets or flooring, so the room is finished when we leave.

Moreno Valley mold removal FAQ

There's a stain on my ceiling under the upstairs bathroom. What causes that?

Usually a leaking shower pan, tub drain, toilet seal or supply line. We find which one it is, fix it, open the ceiling only as much as needed, remove the wet or moldy material and repair the ceiling.

How long do water heaters and washer hoses last?

It varies, but many tank water heaters are replaced somewhere around 10 to 15 years, and rubber washer hoses should be checked regularly and replaced when worn. Braided stainless hoses are a good upgrade.

Do you fix the drywall and paint after removing mold?

Yes. We remove the mold and damaged materials, then rebuild: drywall, texture, paint, baseboards and flooring if needed, so the room is finished when we leave.

What affects the cost of mold removal?

The cost depends on your home: how large the affected area is, which materials are involved (drywall, insulation, cabinets, flooring), how hard it is to reach, whether testing is needed, what it takes to fix the water source, and how much rebuilding is needed afterward. Because no two homes are the same, we don't publish one-size-fits-all prices. We'll visit your home, inspect, and give you personalized pricing with a free on-site estimate. Can I just clean mold with bleach?

On a small spot on a hard surface, like tile, cleaning is often fine. On porous materials like drywall, wood and insulation, bleach doesn't reach the mold growing inside the material, and the moisture problem is still there. The EPA's guidance is to fix the water problem, clean hard surfaces, and remove porous materials that are moldy. For areas larger than about 10 square feet, a professional is recommended.
