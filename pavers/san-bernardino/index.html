Why Pavers Work Well in San Bernardino

San Bernardino's hot dry summers and clay-heavy soils make pavers one of the most practical and durable surface options available.

  • Heat-resistant materials that handle San Bernardino's intense summer temperatures
  • Flexible installation that outperforms solid concrete slabs in shifting soil
  • Good drainage for seasonal rain events
  • Easy individual repairs — no need to replace entire slabs
  • Improved curb appeal that adds value to older and newer properties alike

With proper base prep, pavers hold up well through San Bernardino's dry heat and occasional storms — and look far better long-term than aging concrete.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do pavers last in San Bernardino?

With proper installation and base preparation, pavers can last decades in San Bernardino's hot dry climate.

Can pavers be installed over an existing cracked driveway?

In most cases, we'll remove the old surface and prep the base correctly rather than overlay. This ensures long-term performance and prevents future movement issues.

Are pavers a good upgrade for older San Bernardino homes?

Yes. Pavers are one of the best ways to modernize the exterior of an older home while improving durability and drainage at the same time.
