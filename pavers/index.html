Brick, Concrete & Travertine Pavers

The right paver depends on how the space is used, how hot it gets, and the style of your home.

Brick Pavers

Clay brick keeps its color in desert sun and gives patios, walkways, and borders a classic look that suits Spanish, ranch, and traditional homes. Laid over a compacted base with sand-set joints, individual bricks can be replaced if one cracks.

Concrete Pavers

The widest range of colors, sizes, and patterns, including large-format and brick-look styles. A strong choice for driveways because the interlocking pattern spreads the load and handles slab movement better than one solid pour.

Travertine

Natural stone that stays comfortable underfoot in summer, which makes it popular for pool decks and courtyards. It needs sealing and a well-prepared base. Read the full comparison of concrete, brick, and travertine pavers.

How We Install Pavers

  1. 1. Free on-site estimate

    We measure, check grade and drainage, talk through paver styles and patterns, and give you personalized pricing.

  2. 2. Excavation and grading

    Old concrete, grass, or soil is removed to the right depth and the area is graded so water drains away from the house.

  3. 3. Compacted base

    Base rock is spread and compacted in layers, then screeded with bedding sand. This is what keeps pavers from shifting or sinking.

  4. 4. Laying, cutting, and edging

    Pavers are laid in your chosen pattern, cut clean at borders, and locked in with edge restraints.

  5. 5. Joint sand, sealing, and cleanup

    Polymeric sand is swept into the joints and compacted, optional sealing is applied, and the site is cleaned before we walk it with you.

Paver Installation FAQ

How much does paver installation cost in Palm Springs?

The cost depends on your home: the square footage, removal of existing concrete or grass, base preparation and grading, the paver type and pattern, and site access. Because no two properties are the same, we don't publish one-size-fits-all prices. We'll visit your home, measure, and give you personalized pricing with a free on-site estimate. Call or text (951) 292-0703.

What types of pavers do you install?

We install concrete pavers, brick pavers, travertine, and natural stone pavers for driveways, patios, walkways, pool decks, and courtyards.

Do you install brick pavers?

Yes. We install clay brick pavers for patios, walkways, and borders, and brick-look concrete pavers where you want the style with a wider color range. Brick holds its color well in desert sun and suits Spanish, ranch, and traditional Palm Springs homes.

How long does paver installation take?

It depends on the size of the area, the amount of excavation and base work, and the pattern. We give you a clear timeline with your free on-site estimate, before any work begins.

Do you serve Palm Desert and other areas outside Palm Springs?

Yes. We install pavers for homeowners in Palm Desert, Rancho Mirage, Cathedral City, Indio, Desert Hot Springs, Yucca Valley, Twentynine Palms, Yucaipa, Moreno Valley, Menifee, Murrieta, Temecula, and San Bernardino.
