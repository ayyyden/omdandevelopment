Stamped Concrete Pool Decks in the Coachella Valley

A pool deck in Palm Springs, Palm Desert, or Desert Hot Springs has two jobs: stay comfortable to walk on in summer and stay safe when wet. Stamped concrete gives you the look of stone or tile in one continuous surface, without joints for weeds.

  • Lighter colors and textured patterns that run cooler and grip better when wet
  • Slope set toward deck drains, not toward the pool or the house
  • Control joints placed to manage cracking in desert heat
  • Sealer suited to pool chemicals and sun

Resurfacing an existing deck instead? See our pool remodeling and Cool Deck resurfacing page. Comparing finishes? Read stamped vs. plain concrete for pool decks and what to consider before a concrete pool deck project.

Concrete FAQ

What concrete services do you offer?

We pour and finish concrete for driveways, patios, walkways, steps, curbs, and pool decks, including stamped concrete, broom finish, salt finish, exposed aggregate, and smooth trowel finishes. We work on homes only.

Do you build stamped concrete pool decks?

Yes. We build new stamped concrete pool decks in Palm Springs, Palm Desert, Desert Hot Springs, Rancho Mirage, and across the Coachella Valley, in lighter colors and textured patterns that stay more comfortable and less slippery around the pool.

How much does a concrete driveway or patio cost?

The cost depends on your home: the square footage, demolition of old concrete, base preparation and grading, slab thickness, and the finish you choose. Because no two properties are the same, we don't publish one-size-fits-all prices. We'll visit your home, measure, and give you personalized pricing with a free on-site estimate. Call or text (951) 292-0703.

How long does concrete take to cure?

Concrete is typically walkable within 24–48 hours and reaches full strength in about 28 days. In desert heat, curing is managed carefully so the surface does not dry too fast and crack.
