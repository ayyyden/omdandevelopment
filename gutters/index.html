Gutter Services We Provide

We handle the full range of residential gutter work across Palm Springs and the surrounding valley.

New Gutter Installation

We install seamless aluminum gutters cut on-site for a precise fit. No seams along straight runs means fewer leak points and a cleaner look. We size the gutters to match the roof area and expected rainfall load.

Gutter Repair

Leaking joints, gutters pulling away from the fascia, bent sections, and blocked downspouts are common repair calls. We diagnose the problem and fix it cleanly — including resealing joints, re-securing hangers, and clearing blockages.

Gutter Replacement

If your existing gutters are rusted through, severely damaged, or have been repaired multiple times with diminishing results, replacement is the cleaner long-term solution. We remove the old system and install new seamless gutters in its place.

Downspout Installation & Routing

Where the water goes after it leaves the gutter matters. We position and route downspouts to direct runoff away from foundations, hardscape, and landscaping. We also extend downspouts when the standard drop point puts water too close to the house.

Drainage Support

For homes with low-lying areas, uneven grading, or hardscape that directs water toward the house, we can discuss drainage options that work with your gutter system to route water where it should go — away from the home and foundation.

Fascia & Soffit Assessment

Before installing gutters we check the fascia boards they'll attach to. If the fascia has moisture damage or rot from years of improper drainage, we can address it as part of the project so the new gutters have a solid, dry surface to attach to.

Why gutters matter in a desert climate

It's a fair question — if Palm Springs only gets about five inches of rain per year, why worry about gutters? The answer is how that rain falls. The desert doesn't get steady drizzle. It gets short, intense storms, often in late summer and early fall, that can drop an inch or more in a matter of hours. Rooflines concentrate all of that water into a fast-moving sheet that has to go somewhere.

Without gutters, that sheet of water drops off the roofline and hits the soil, stucco, and hardscape directly below the eave. Over time this erodes soil near the foundation, stains exterior walls, deteriorates stucco, and can cause moisture intrusion in older homes. It also damages fascia boards — the wood behind the gutter line — which are expensive to repair if left to rot.

For homes in Palm Desert, Cathedral City, Indio, La Quinta, and other Coachella Valley communities, the story is similar. Desert Hot Springs, Yucca Valley, and Twentynine Palms can see even heavier seasonal storms given their elevation. Homes in Yucaipa, Moreno Valley, Menifee, Murrieta, Temecula, and San Bernardino — communities that get more regular rainfall — benefit even more from proper gutter systems.

Vacation homes and HOA properties have an additional consideration: no one may be there to notice damage accumulating between visits. A working gutter system reduces the maintenance burden and protects the property year-round without requiring attention.

Protecting your fascia

Fascia boards sit right at the gutter line. Without gutters, or with gutters that have pulled away from the fascia, water runs down the board face every storm. Rot follows. Replacing rotted fascia is more work and cost than the gutter installation that would have prevented it.

Foundation runoff

Desert soil can be compacted and slow to absorb water. When heavy rainfall hits the ground right at the roofline edge, it pools against the foundation instead of soaking in. Gutters route that water to a downspout and away from the structure.

Exterior paint and stucco

Stucco and exterior paint degrade faster when water repeatedly runs down the face of the wall. Gutters keep the water off the wall and reduce the frequency of exterior repainting and stucco repair.

Vacation and rental homes

If you're not there every week, gutters are a low-cost form of insurance. They work passively to protect the property every time it rains — no action required on your part between visits.

Gutter FAQ

Common questions about gutter installation and repair in Palm Springs and the Coachella Valley.

Do homes in Palm Springs really need gutters?

Yes. Even though the desert gets less annual rainfall than most of California, the storms that do hit Palm Springs and the Coachella Valley can drop a lot of water in a short window. Without gutters, that runoff hits the fascia, foundation, and landscaping at full force. For vacation homes and rental properties — where no one may be on-site during a storm — gutters provide important passive protection between visits.

How do I know if my gutters need repair or full replacement?

Signs that point to repair: small leaks at joints, a single section pulling away from the fascia, or a downspout that's come loose. Signs that point to replacement: gutters that are severely rusted, badly bent, or sagging across multiple sections, or older aluminum gutters that have been patched multiple times. We'll give you an honest assessment when we look at the job — no upsell pressure.

Can you install gutters on older or remodeled homes?

Yes. We work on a wide range of homes including older construction, recently remodeled exteriors, and homes that have had fascia or roofline work done. We measure and cut seamless gutters on-site so they fit your roofline cleanly regardless of the home's age or layout.

Do you help with downspouts and drainage routing?

Yes. Proper downspout placement is part of every gutter installation we do. We route downspouts to direct water away from the foundation, hardscape, and landscaping. If the yard has drainage challenges, we can discuss options for where water should go so it doesn't pool near the home.

Do you provide gutter services for rental or vacation homes?

Yes. A good portion of our gutter work is on investment and vacation properties throughout Palm Springs, Palm Desert, and the surrounding Coachella Valley. We work efficiently and communicate clearly so owners can get the job done without being on-site.

How long does gutter installation take?

Most residential gutter installations are completed in a single day. Larger homes or projects that include downspout rerouting or fascia repair may take two days. We give you a timeline estimate when we quote the job so you know what to expect.
